3. Hosting

This website is hosted by Render Services, Inc. (525 Brannan Street, Suite 300, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the website, Render automatically collects information in so-called server log files that your browser transmits: IP address, date and time of access, page accessed, browser type and operating system. This data is technically required to deliver the website and to ensure its stability and security (legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR – legitimate interest in the secure provision of the website).

Render also processes data in the USA. The transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

4. Fonts (locally hosted)

For a consistent appearance, this website uses fonts that are stored locally on our server. When you visit the site, no connection to servers of Google or other font providers is established, and no personal data is transferred to third parties in this process.

5. Embedded content (images and videos)

Images and videos on this website are delivered via the content delivery network of Framer B.V. (Rozengracht 207B, 1016 LZ Amsterdam, Netherlands). When this content is loaded, your IP address is transmitted to Framer's servers (legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR – legitimate interest in the performant presentation of our content).

We embed sample and reference videos via Wistia, Inc. (120 Brookline Street, Cambridge, MA 02139, USA). When these videos are loaded or played, your IP address is transmitted to Wistia; the transfer to the USA is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). For more information, see Wistia's privacy policy.

The articles on our blog page and the customer reviews on the home and results pages are embedded via widgets of the service Sternfänger (Cleves & Löb Sternfänger GbR, Brunnenstraße 1, 65594 Wirbelau, Germany), technically delivered via the MyLocalBusiness platform (embed.mylocalbusiness.io). When you visit these pages, your IP address is transmitted to this provider's servers to deliver the content (legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR – legitimate interest in providing our blog content). For more information, see Sternfänger's privacy policy.

6. Contact form and getting in touch

If you contact us via the contact form, by email or by phone, the details you provide (name, email address, phone number, message and – voluntarily – your company) are stored by us to process your inquiry and for possible follow-up questions. We do not pass this data on without your consent.

Processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in processing your inquiry). Your data is deleted once your inquiry has been fully processed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

For the technical delivery of the form data we use the service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Your entries are transmitted to FormSubmit and forwarded to us from there by email. For more information, see the provider's website.

7. Cookies, consent and tracking

This website only uses analytics and marketing services with your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). On your first visit, a banner asks whether you agree. Without consent, the services listed below are not loaded and no marketing cookies are set. Your choice is stored locally in your browser and can be changed at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

Google Tag Manager

With your consent, we use Google Tag Manager (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), through which analytics and marketing tags are managed and loaded. In this process your IP address is transmitted to Google; processing in the USA is possible (basis: EU-US Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses). For more information, see Google's privacy policy.

Meta Pixel

With your consent, we use the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland) to measure the effectiveness of our ads (e.g. page views and submitted inquiries). Data may be transmitted to Meta and processed in the USA (basis: EU-US Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses). For more information, see Meta's privacy policy.

You can revoke your consent at any time with effect for the future – via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

8. Your rights

Within the scope of applicable law, you have the right at any time to:

  • access to your stored personal data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to the processing (Art. 21 GDPR).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

For any questions about your data, an informal email to info@jaeger-shot.de is all it takes.

9. Currency of this policy

This privacy policy is current as of August 2026. As our website evolves, it may become necessary to update this policy. This English version is provided for convenience; in case of discrepancies, the German version at jaeger-shot.de/datenschutz prevails.
