Frequently asked questions

Corporate video questions.

How long does it take to produce a corporate video?
From the free consultation to the finished film usually takes a few weeks, depending on scope. We set the exact timeline together — and then we actually stick to it. As a two-person team, we decide fast, with no approval loops across five departments.
How long should a corporate video be?
Shorter than most people think. Two to three minutes is almost always enough to build trust without boring anyone. On request, we also cut short versions from the footage for social media, so your film works in the feed too.
Do we have to get in front of the camera and recite lines?
No. The strongest corporate videos show you doing your work, not reciting sentences. If someone wants to speak, we guide them through a relaxed conversation and cut the best moments from it. Nobody has to memorize anything.
Do we own the film afterwards?
Yes. You get the finished film to use on all your channels, with licensed music. No hidden follow-up costs, no surprises.
