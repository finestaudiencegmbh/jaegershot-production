Honest answers

The questions you are probably asking yourself.

Do I have to be on camera myself?
Only if you want to. Many of our best videos work without the boss saying a word: insights into the business, the team at work, finished projects. And if you do want to be on camera: we guide you through everything. You do not need any camera experience.
How much of my own time does this take?
A lot less than you think. After the consultation, all you really need to do is schedule the shoot days at your place. Concept, filming, editing and polishing are on us. Your business keeps running while we work.
Am I tied to long contracts?
That only applies to our monthly packages – and even there you choose the term yourself: 3, 6 or 12 months. Longer terms are rewarded with better prices, but nobody gets pushed into a contract they do not want. And one-off projects like a corporate video or event video run without any term at all: one job, one price, done.
How fast do I get my videos?
With a monthly package, you reliably get your content every month, fully edited and ready to use. For one-off projects we agree on the timeline in the consultation and then stick to it. Short lines of communication are no empty phrase in a two-person team.
We have worked with an agency before. It got us nothing. Why would you be any different?
We hear this a lot, and it usually comes down to three things: changing contacts, long contracts and empty promises. We deliberately do all three differently. You always work with the same two people, you choose your term yourself, and we do not promise anything we cannot deliver. In the consultation we will also tell you honestly if video content is not the right investment for you right now.
Which industries do you work for?
We film for skilled trades, restaurants, car dealerships, real estate agents and service businesses in Aschaffenburg, the Rhine-Main area and Lower Franconia – and across Germany on request. What matters is not your industry, but that you want to be visible on a regular basis. Whether your business is a fit is something we will clarify honestly in the consultation.
